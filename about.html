ABOUT ME

Get to Know Me

Customer Service, Sales, Administration, Leadership, and Community Engagement.

My name is Katrinah W. M. Tulay, and I am a motivated, dependable, and hardworking individual with practical experience in customer service and sales. I am passionate about helping people, building positive customer relationships, and providing professional assistance. I take pride in my ability to communicate effectively, understand people's needs, and work collaboratively to achieve shared goals.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology with a minor in Political Science at the University of Liberia. My academic background complements my interest in understanding people, human relationships, and society. I also hold a High School Diploma from Dekwowah International School of Excellence, where I graduated in 2023.

My professional experience includes working as a Customer Service Representative at Orange Liberia and as a Salesperson at China Mall. These roles helped me develop practical skills in customer relations, product promotion, problem-solving, teamwork, and professional communication. They also strengthened my ability to adapt to new environments, follow workplace procedures, and approach responsibilities with patience and professionalism.

Beyond my professional experience, I served as the first President of the YourMind Initiative, a student-led initiative focused on personal development, leadership, advocacy, mental awareness, and community empowerment. This experience strengthened my leadership, public speaking, organization, planning, and decision-making skills while allowing me to engage and motivate other students.

I consider myself a fast learner, a strong communicator, a reliable team player, and an emerging leader who is always willing to learn and take on new responsibilities. My goal is to contribute my customer service, sales, communication, leadership, and organizational skills to a reputable organization while continuing to grow personally and professionally.
